ingredients:
  - item: Frozen Chopped Spinach
    prep: thawed and water squeezed out
    quantity: 2
    unit: Bags
  - item: Eggs
    quantity: 3
  - item: Yellow Onion
    prep: chopped (or more)
    quantity: 0.5
    unit: Cup
  - item: Red Bell Pepper
    prep: chopped, optional
    quantity: 0.5
    unit: Cup
  - item: Garlic Powder
    prep: up to ½ t, depending on your taste
    quantity: 0.25
    unit: Teaspoon
  - item: Bread Crumbs
    prep: or more as needed
    quantity: 0.5
    unit: Cup
  - item: Parmesan Cheese
    prep: grated (green canister kind), or more as needed
    quantity: 0.5
    unit: Cup
  - item: Olive Oil
    prep: for sautéing and frying
    quantity: 2
    unit: Tablespoon
---
1. Sauté the {Yellow Onion} in {Olive Oil 1/2} until lightly brown. If using the {Red Bell Pepper}, sauté it with the onion.
2. Squeeze all the water out of the thawed {Frozen Chopped Spinach} and place it in a large bowl.
3. Push the spinach over to one side of the bowl, break the {Eggs} on the other side of the bowl and beat them with a fork, then incorporate them together.
4. Add in all the rest of the ingredients (the sautéed onion, {Garlic Powder}, {Bread Crumbs}, and {Parmesan Cheese}) and mix thoroughly. It should be of the consistency to be made into patties and hold together.
5. Form into patties (about 10) and fry in the remaining {Olive Oil} over medium heat, about 7 min on each side. I usually cover them for most of the cooking time.
6. I serve this with steamed baby carrots and egg noodles. Bruce likes his spinach burger with a little mayo on top.
