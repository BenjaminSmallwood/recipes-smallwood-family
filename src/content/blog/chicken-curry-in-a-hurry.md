  - item: Salt

    unit: Teaspoon
  - item: Curry Powder
    quantity: 2

  - item: Coconut Milk

    quantity: 1
    unit: Cup

    unit: Tablespoon
  - item: Chicken
    prep: boneless skinless breast, cut into 1 in cubes
    quantity: 1
    unit: lb
  - item: Spinach
    prep: fresh baby, packed
    quantity: 3
    unit: Cups
  - item: Rice
    prep: uncooked, for serving
    quantity: 1
    unit: Cup
---
1. Heat the {Olive Oil} in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the {Yellow Onion} and {Salt}. Cook the onion, stirring often, until soft (about 7 minutes).
2. Add the {Curry Powder} and continue stirring 1 min. more.
3. Stir in the {Coconut Milk}, {Diced Tomatoes}, and {Tomato Paste}. Continue cooking the mixture, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes or until the sauce thickens slightly.
4. Add the {Chicken}, stir well, and cook for 5 to 6 min. or until the meat is cooked through.
5. Add the {Spinach} and cook, stirring occasionally, until wilted, about 3 minutes. Add more salt to taste.
6. Cook the {Rice} and serve the curry warm over it. In my opinion, it tastes best warm, not hot.
