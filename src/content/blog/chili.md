  - item: Ground Cumin
    quantity: 1

  - item: Worcestershire Sauce
    quantity: 1
    unit: tsp
---
1. In a large pot brown and crumble {Ground Beef} with {Celery}, {Yellow Onion}, and {Garlic} over medium high heat (until meat is completely brown and vegetables are tender.) Season with a little salt and pepper, to taste
2. Drain excess grease.
3. Open cans of {Petite Diced Tomatoes}, {Tomato Sauce}, {Dark Red Kidney Beans}, {Beef Broth}, and {Black Beans} and dump the content of those into the pot.
4. Add in {Chili Powder}, {Ground Cumin} and {Worcestershire Sauce} and mix thoroughly. Add salt and pepper, to taste. Cover with lid and cook on low till integrated. Remove lid and let steam until desired thickness.
5. Taste before serving and add more salt and pepper if necessary. Top each serving with your favorite toppings like: shredded cheddar cheese, chopped onions, green onions sour cream.
