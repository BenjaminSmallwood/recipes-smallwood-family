1. Put the {Chicken} in the bottom of the slow cooker and season it with {Salt}, {Black Pepper}, {Ground Cumin}, {Dried Oregano}, {Chili Powder} and {Cayenne Pepper}.
2. Top with {Yellow Onion}, {Garlic}, {Great Northern Beans}, {Diced Green Chiles}, {Corn}, {Chicken Broth} and {Cilantro}, then stir.
3. Cover and cook on LOW for 8 hours or on HIGH for 3-4 hours.
4. Move the chicken to a large bowl, shred it, then return it to the slow cooker.
5. Stir in {Cream Cheese} and {Half and Half}, cover, and cook on HIGH for 15 minutes, until the chili is creamy and slightly thickened. For the smoothest sauce, first whisk the softened cream cheese with a few ladles of hot chili in a bowl, then stir that back in.
6. Stir well and serve with toppings like sliced jalapeños, avocado, sour cream, fresh cilantro, tortilla strips, and shredded Monterey Jack or Mexican cheese.
