1. Pat the {Chicken} dry with a paper towel and cut it into cubes.
2. Heat {Olive Oil} in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Once the oil is hot, add the chicken and cook for 2-3 minutes, until the edges start to cook.
4. Add {Sun-Dried Tomatoes}, {Garlic}, {Italian Seasoning}, {Paprika}, {Kosher Salt} and {Black Pepper}, and cook for 3-4 more minutes.
5. Stir in {Orzo} and {Chicken Broth}.
6. Reduce the heat to medium, cover, and cook for 12 minutes, stirring every few minutes so the orzo doesn't stick to the bottom of the pan.
7. Stir in {Heavy Cream} and {Spinach} until the spinach wilts.
8. Stir in {Parmesan Cheese}, remove from the heat, and serve right away.
