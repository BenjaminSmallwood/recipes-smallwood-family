  You can eat these in a boat, you can eat these with a goat, you can eat these here or there, you can eat these anywhere; but they are especially good with a hot cup of coffee and a good friend. (Or alone with a glass of water. Or in the desert as your last meal. Or as your only source of nutrition. ☺) Enjoy!

  Note: This recipe, because of the volume, lends itself well to being made in a Kitchen Aid or Bosch. It can be mixed by hand, I did it that way for years, it will give you a work out. Also, this is an excellent recipe, but if you want excellent results you must follow the directions exactly. It is not a recipe you can fudge on. Don't use generic ingredients and don't skip the parchment paper! If you do, they will still taste good, but won't look as good.

1. In a large mixing bowl or in the bowl of your Kitchen Aid or Bosch, cream {Butter}, {Molasses}, {Water}, {Salt}, and {Brown Sugar} together. Beat in {Eggs}.
2. In a separate bowl sift the dry ingredients together ({Flour}, {Baking Soda}, {Cinnamon}, {Ground Cloves}, and {Ground Ginger}), add to the cream mixture, and mix well.
3. Refrigerate 24 hours (or at least 8 hrs.)
4. Roll into 2 inch balls, then roll in {Granulated Sugar}. Place on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper.
5. Bake at 350° for 15-18 minutes. Yields 3 ½ dozen.
